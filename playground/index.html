    M2: the same architecture at three sizes, evaluated on held-out words — the split is by Persian word before Finglish variants are generated, so no test word appears in training under any spelling.

    No npm package converts Finglish to Persian. Every package whose name says finglish or pinglish goes the other way — Persian to Latin, for URL slugs. Worldwide there are a handful of real implementations and exactly one other is JavaScript. So this is less “us versus the competition” than a survey of what exists and why almost none of it runs in a browser.

    Size

    Not benchmarked, and why

    Confidence is the length-normalized likelihood of the best path, so a long word is not penalized for being long. Protected spans — URLs, emails, @mentions, numbers, code, English — pass through byte-identical and are never scored.
