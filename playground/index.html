    Spans

    Every span shows what happened to it and why. Converted spans list each candidate the decoder considered with its probability.

    Confidence is the length-normalized likelihood of the best path, so a long word is not penalized for being long. Protected spans — URLs, emails, @mentions, numbers, code, English — pass through byte-identical and are never scored.
