# Chat words: a sentence containing one of these, and none of the `!` words, is
# drawn by `build_distill.py --targeted-predicate chat-word`. One word per line,
# compared after normalize() with ZWNJ removed, so میخوام also matches می‌خوام.
#
# Chosen as markers of chat register, not of Persian in general: every entry is
# either a greeting or reply formula, a colloquial verb or pronoun form that
# written Persian spells differently (میخوام / می‌خواهم), or a chat loanword.
# Measured on HomoRich before this list was cut down: شده, کن, بکن, چرا,
# داریم, زنگ, خسته, دلم and حوصله each admitted thousands of read-aloud
# sentences, and اره is as often "saw" as "yeah", so they are not here.
#
# `!` lines are the other half: a sentence containing one is written register
# and is not drawn even if it has a chat word. خوبی is the case that needs it -
# "به خوبی ... می‌کند" is its commonest use in HomoRich.
#
